The Mercantile Athletics Federation proudly invites your esteemed organisation to partner with us for the 41st Mercantile Annual Athletics Championship 2026 and the Mercantile Athletics Awards Night 2025.

For over four decades, MAF has stood as Sri Lanka's leading platform for athletics, corporate wellness, teamwork and sporting excellence within the mercantile sector.

The 2025 edition will be conducted on a grand scale — with enhanced digital exposure, corporate activations, live entertainment and nationwide media visibility — across two flagship occasions held at the country's most prestigious venues.

This proposal outlines a curated suite of sponsorship opportunities, each designed to maximise brand exposure, employee engagement and corporate recognition through one of Sri Lanka's most celebrated corporate sporting events.

We warmly welcome your partnership in making this a resounding success.

— 04 — Criteria of Recognition
Awards Criteria

The standards of
distinction.

Three categories of recognition, each honouring a different measure of mercantile athletic excellence. Eligible athletes are listed in the federation's official selection documents.

· i ·

Gold
Award

Presented to athletes who have
  • Achieved any three (3) Gold Medals
  • Set a Meet Record
  • Been adjudged as Meet Champion
· ii ·

Silver
Award

Presented to athletes who have
  • Secured two (2) Gold Medals in individual events
  • Won any three (3) medals
· iii ·

Overseas
Tour Award

Presented to athletes who have

Represented Sri Lanka through the Mercantile Athletics Federation in international competition.
